Info for visitors

Tap a place for directions from the Main Gate.

  • Getting in: Follow the main path from the Main Gate to the building entrance. The Statue and IOIT are near the gate; the Cop and the Garden path come off further along.
  • Finding rooms: Rooms 100–106 and 141 are close to the entrance. The 114–125 rooms are along the main and cross corridors, and 126–136 are towards the far end.
  • Stairs and lift: Stairs-1 is just inside the entrance. Stairs-2 and WR are at the top of the main corridor, Stairs-3 and LWR at the far end, and the lift is next to room 100.
  • Closed gate: The gate off the bottom corridor is closed, so please head back to the main entrance to leave.
  • Outside the building: The Canteen and the Garden are both reached by a short flight of steps from the main path.
